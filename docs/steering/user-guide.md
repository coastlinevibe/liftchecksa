## Getting Started

### What is LiftCheck S.A?

LiftCheck S.A is a safety verification platform for South African lift clubs. We help you verify drivers before you pay or travel, reducing the risk of scams in Facebook and WhatsApp lift groups.

**Important**: LiftCheck S.A is NOT a transport marketplace. We don't arrange lifts or handle payments between drivers and passengers. We only verify identities and provide a safety layer for your existing lift groups.

### How It Works

1. **Drivers** get verified with ID, licence, and vehicle checks
2. **Drivers** create trips and share links to Facebook/WhatsApp groups
3. **Passengers** click the link to view driver verification and vehicle details
4. **Passengers** book through the app and confirm before traveling
5. **Both parties** rate each other after the trip

### Creating an Account

#### Step 1: Choose Your Account Type

Visit [liftcheck.co.za](https://liftcheck.co.za) and choose:

- **Find a Lift** - For passengers (R36/year Basic or R96/year Plus)
- **Offer a Lift** - For drivers (R300/year Verified Provider)
- **Manage a Group** - For Facebook group admins (Free)

#### Step 2: Fill in Your Details

- First name
- Surname
- Phone number
- Email address
- Password (minimum 6 characters)
- Home province (optional for members, helps with route matching)

#### Step 3: Complete Payment

After registration, you'll receive a unique payment reference:

- **Basic Member**: LC-M-xxxxx (R36)
- **Plus Member**: LC-P-xxxxx (R96)
- **Driver**: LC-D-xxxxx (R300)

**Bank Details:**
- Bank: FNB
- Account: LiftCheck Safety
- Account No: 62812345678
- Branch: 250655

#### Step 4: Upload Proof of Payment

1. Make your EFT payment using your reference number
2. Take a screenshot or photo of your payment confirmation
3. Upload it through the app
4. Wait for approval (usually within 24 hours)

## For Passengers (Members)

### Finding a Lift

#### Method 1: Browse Available Trips

1. Go to **"Find Trips"** in the app
2. Filter by:
   - Origin city
   - Destination city
   - Date range
   - Price range
3. View trip details including:
   - Driver verification status
   - Vehicle details
   - Ratings and reviews
   - Available seats
   - Cost per seat

#### Method 2: Click Shared Links

1. Driver shares trip link in Facebook/WhatsApp group
2. Click the link to view trip details
3. See driver's verified ID card
4. Check vehicle photos and details
5. Read previous passenger reviews

### Viewing Driver Profiles

When you view a trip, you'll see the driver's **ID Card** showing:

- ✅ Verified badge (green checkmark)
- First name and surname
- Profile photo
- Star rating (out of 5)
- Number of completed trips
- Verification status:
  - ID Document ✅
  - Driver's Licence ✅
  - Vehicle Registration ✅

### Booking a Trip

1. Click **"Request Seat"** on the trip page
2. Optionally add a message to the driver
3. Specify your pickup and dropoff points (if different from default)
4. Wait for driver to accept your request
5. You'll receive a notification when accepted

### Before the Trip

#### Match Check (Safety Feature)

On the day of travel:

1. Open the app and go to **"My Trips"**
2. Click **"Match Check"** for your trip
3. Compare the displayed information with the actual driver and vehicle:
   - Driver's face matches photo
   - Vehicle make, model, and color match
   - Licence plate matches
4. If everything matches, click **"Confirm Match"**
5. If something doesn't match, click **"Report Issue"**

#### Bluetooth Verification (Offline Verification)

If you don't have data or signal:

1. Enable Bluetooth on your phone
2. Open LiftCheck S.A app
3. Go to **"Bluetooth Verify"**
4. Hold your phone near the driver's phone
5. The app will verify the driver's identity via Bluetooth
6. You'll see a green checkmark if verified

### During the Trip

#### Safety Check-In

1. Once the trip starts, click **"Check In"**
2. This notifies your emergency contact (if set up)
3. Your location is recorded (optional)

#### In-App Chat

- Message the driver through the app
- Share pickup location details
- Coordinate meeting points
- Keep all communication in the app for safety

### After the Trip

#### Rate Your Driver

1. Go to **"Trip History"**
2. Click on the completed trip
3. Rate the driver (1-5 stars)
4. Add feedback (optional)
5. Select tags:
   - On time
   - Safe driving
   - Friendly
   - Clean vehicle
   - Good music
   - Quiet ride

#### Report Issues

If you experienced problems:

1. Go to **"Report"** in the app
2. Select report type:
   - Took money, then blocked me
   - Fake driver (wrong person)
   - Wrong vehicle
   - No show
   - Unsafe driving
   - Harassment
   - Overcharging
3. Provide details and evidence
4. Upload screenshots or photos
5. Submit report for admin review

### Plus Member Features (R96/year)

#### Trip Share

- Save your favorite routes
- Get alerts when new trips match your routes
- Share your trip plans with trusted drivers
- Priority support

#### Trusted Drivers List

- Mark drivers you trust

## For Drivers (Providers)

### Getting Verified

#### Step 1: Upload Documents

After registration and payment, upload:

1. **ID Document**
   - Clear photo of your SA ID book or card
   - All details must be visible
   - No glare or shadows

2. **Driver's Licence**
   - Front and back of licence card
   - Must be valid (not expired)
   - All details legible

3. **Vehicle Documents**
   - Vehicle registration (licence disc)
   - Must match your vehicle details
   - Must be current

4. **Proof of Address** (optional but recommended)
   - Recent utility bill or bank statement
   - Within last 3 months

#### Step 2: Wait for Verification

- Admin reviews your documents (24-48 hours)
- You'll receive email notification
- Check app for verification status
- If rejected, you can resubmit with corrections

### Adding Your Vehicle

1. Go to **"My Vehicles"**
2. Click **"Add Vehicle"**
3. Enter details:
   - Make (e.g., Toyota)
   - Model (e.g., Corolla)
   - Color (e.g., White)
   - Licence plate (e.g., CA 123-456)
   - Year (optional)
4. Upload photos:
   - Front view
   - Side view
   - Interior (optional)
   - Licence disc
5. Submit for verification

### Creating a Trip

#### Step 1: Route Details

1. Go to **"Create Trip"**
2. Enter:
   - **From**: Origin city (e.g., Cape Town)
   - **To**: Destination city (e.g., Mthatha)
   - **Pickup Point**: Specific location (e.g., Corner of Adderley & Strand)
   - **Dropoff Point**: Specific location (e.g., Mthatha Plaza)
   - **Date**: Departure date
   - **Time**: Departure time

#### Step 2: Pricing & Seats

1. Set **Price per Seat**: Cost-share amount (e.g., R350)
2. Select **Available Seats**: 1-4 seats
3. Choose **Vehicle**: Select from your verified vehicles

#### Step 3: Trip Rules

1. **Luggage Allowance**:
   - 1 small bag per person
   - 1 medium bag per person
   - 1 large bag per person
   - No luggage

2. **Passenger Rules** (optional):
   - No smoking
   - No loud music
   - Be on time
   - Respectful behavior

3. **Additional Notes** (optional):
   - Stops along the way
   - Preferred payment method
   - Special requirements

#### Step 4: Publish & Share

1. Click **"Publish Trip"**
2. You'll receive a shareable link
3. Copy the link
4. Share in your Facebook/WhatsApp lift groups
5. Passengers can click to view and book

### Managing Trip Requests

1. Go to **"Trip Requests"**
2. View pending requests with passenger details:
   - Name and surname
   - Rating (if they've traveled before)
   - Message from passenger
   - Pickup/dropoff preferences
3. For each request:
   - **Accept**: Confirm the booking
   - **Reject**: Decline with optional reason
   - **Message**: Chat with passenger

### Before the Trip

#### Confirm Passengers

1. Go to **"My Trips"**
2. View accepted passengers
3. Send reminder messages
4. Share final pickup details

#### Match Check

- Passengers will verify your identity
- Ensure your vehicle matches the description
- Be at the pickup point on time

### During the Trip

#### Check-In Passengers

1. When passengers arrive, verify their identity
2. Use **Bluetooth Verification** if needed
3. Mark passengers as **"Checked In"**

#### Safety

- Be respectful and professional
- Keep communication in the app

### After the Trip

#### Rate Passengers

1. Rate each passenger (1-5 stars)
2. Add feedback (optional)
3. Select tags:
   - On time

### Building Your Reputation

## Troubleshooting

### Account Issues

**Q: I forgot my password**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page, enter your email, and follow the reset link.

**Q: My payment hasn't been approved**
A: Check that:
- Payment reference matches exactly
- Proof of payment is clear
- Amount is correct
- Wait 24-48 hours for review

**Q: I can't log in**
A: Ensure:
- Email is correct
- Password is correct (case-sensitive)
- Account is activated
- Internet connection is stable

### Verification Issues

**Q: My documents were rejected**
A: Common reasons:
- Photo is blurry or unclear
- Document is expired
- Details don't match registration
- Glare or shadows obscure text
Resubmit with better quality photos.

**Q: How long does verification take?**
A: Usually 24-48 hours. Check your email for updates.

### Trip Issues

**Q: Driver hasn't accepted my request**
A: Drivers have 24 hours to respond. If no response, try another trip.

**Q: Driver cancelled my trip**
A: You'll be notified. Find another trip or contact support.

**Q: I need to cancel my booking**
A: Go to "My Trips", select the trip, click "Cancel Booking". Do this as soon as possible.

### Safety Concerns

**Q: The driver doesn't match the photo**
A: DO NOT get in the vehicle. Report immediately through the app.

**Q: The vehicle is different**
A: DO NOT get in. Report the issue and find alternative transport.

**Q: I feel unsafe during the trip**
A: Use the in-app emergency button or call 112 (emergency services).

### Technical Issues

**Q: App won't load**
A: 
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser
- Update your browser

**Q: Bluetooth Verification not working**
A: 
- Enable Bluetooth on both phones
- Ensure both users have the app open
- Hold phones close together (within 1 meter)
- Try again

**Q: Can't upload photos**
A:
- Check file size (max 5MB)
- Use JPG or PNG format
- Ensure camera permissions are enabled
- Try different photo

🚩 Driver/passenger has no ratings or reviews
🚩 Profile photo looks fake or stock image
🚩 Requests payment before trip

🚩 Driver/passenger is evasive about details
🚩 Price is too good to be true
🚩 Pressure to book immediately
🚩 Multiple negative reviews
🚩 Verification status is "pending" or "rejected"

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is LiftCheck a taxi service?**
A: No, we're a verification platform for lift clubs. We don't arrange transport or handle payments between drivers and passengers.

**Q: Do I need to pay the driver through the app?**
A: No, payment arrangements are between you and the driver (usually EFT or cash). LiftCheck only charges for membership.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I cancel?**
A: Membership fees are non-refundable. Trip cancellations are between you and the driver.

**Q: Is my personal information safe?**
A: Yes, we show your first name and surname publicly. Full details are only shared after booking confirmation.

**Q: What if I have a dispute with a driver/passenger?**
A: Use the in-app report system. Our admin team will review and mediate.

**Q: Can I use LiftCheck S.A without internet?**
A: Limited features work offline (Bluetooth Verification). Most features require internet.

**Q: Do you operate outside South Africa?**
A: Currently, LiftCheck S.A is only available in South Africa.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact support@liftcheck.co.za with your request. Account deletion is permanent.
